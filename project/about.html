Behind the Channel

About PsychKy

I am the creator behind PsychKy Gaming. I built the channel around something I genuinely enjoy: turning League of Legends games into entertaining challenges and finding out what happens when unusual ideas are taken seriously.

My Story

Why I Started PsychKy Gaming

Gaming has always been more fun to me when there is a challenge attached to it. Instead of only playing the strongest or most common strategy, I enjoy testing strange ideas and seeing how far they can go.

PsychKy gives me a place to combine that interest with editing, storytelling, humor, and the process of building a community around the games I enjoy.

From Idea to Upload

How a PsychKy Video Comes Together

  1. 1

    Choose the Challenge

    I take champs suggested by my viewers, and put them in the jungle.

  2. 2

    Play the Games

    Put the idea through real matches and let the wins, losses, and mistakes shape the story.

  3. 3

    Edit the Story

    Cut the matches down to the moments that are funny, important, or necessary to understand the challenge.

  4. 4

    Share It

    Publish the finished video and use viewer reactions to help inspire what comes next.
